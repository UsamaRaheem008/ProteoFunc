🏗️ System Design

Model Architecture

A dual-encoder multimodal system that fuses protein sequence representations with PPI network topology for comprehensive function prediction.

🧬 Protein Sequence
🕸️ PPI Graph
ESM-2 (650M)
GraphSAGE

📊 MLP Classifier -> GO Terms

🧬 Sequence Encoder

Pre-trained ESM-2 t33 650M extracts rich per-residue representations. A projection head with LayerNorm -> Linear(1280->512) -> GELU maps embeddings to the shared latent space.

🕸️ Graph Encoder

GraphSAGE with 2 layers and mean aggregation learns neighborhood-aware representations from the protein-protein interaction network. Hidden dim: 256.

⚡ Attention Fusion

Multi-head cross-attention (4 heads) over concatenated [seq ‖ net] representations. Learns dynamic modality weighting per protein.

📊 Classification Head

MLP with [512->256->num_GO] architecture using GELU activation, LayerNorm, and Dropout. Trained with Focal Loss (γ=2) using AdamW optimizer with Cosine LR scheduling.

🏷️ Gene Ontology

Three Namespaces of Function

Proteins are annotated across three complementary ontological domains, each describing a different aspect of biological function.

⚗️

Molecular Function

MF

What a protein does at the molecular level - its biochemical activity (e.g., binding, catalysis).

GO:0005524 - ATP binding
GO:0004674 - Protein serine/threonine kinase
GO:0003700 - Transcription factor activity
🔄

Biological Process

BP

The broader biological program a protein contributes to - pathways and cellular programs.

GO:0006468 - Protein phosphorylation
GO:0006915 - Apoptotic process
GO:0007165 - Signal transduction
🏠

Cellular Component

CC

Where in the cell a protein resides or operates - its subcellular localization.

GO:0005634 - Nucleus
GO:0005737 - Cytoplasm
GO:0016020 - Membrane
